# Calculation and evidence rules

The cutoff is a fixed UTC instant. Current balances use the last chain block at or before that instant; record chain, block number/hash, timestamp, price timestamp and source. Reject observations after cutoff and current balance/valuation observations older than 24 hours. Retrieved timestamps can follow cutoff, but facts, observations, budget approvals and merged/completed events must not. Historical queries of mutable GitHub/organizational records require reviewing edit dates and excluding later revisions.

Use twelve completed calendar months followed by a separately labeled MTD interval. Intervals are [start, end). A quarter is complete only when all three completed months are covered; the current quarter is QTD and a leading truncated historical quarter is labeled partial coverage. Never compare a partial quarter to a complete quarter as if equivalent.

## Vault scope and deduplication

Identify vaults by chain ID and lowercased address. Declare all exclusions with reasons, including economically unrecoverable bridged assets and pre-deposit placeholders without invested capital. Include empty eligible vaults with observed zero balances. A fully matched cohort may consistently exclude candidates with unresolved historical/current valuation, but must disclose all exclusions, registry counts and unknown excluded capital; a count percentage is not TVL coverage. Keep the same time-varying eligible universe (activation at or before observation) for TVL, vault fees, flows, depositors and concentration; disclose any narrower secondary scope.

Every current/daily holding contains gross USD and overlap edges into other included holdings. Each edge is an independently valued claim counted twice: vault debt to another vault, wrapper shares or curation capital already included. Subtract each overlap once. Do not add curation headline TVL to vault TVL without reconciling intersecting capital. Reject duplicate edges, cycles and overlaps exceeding either side's holding. Use historical overlap evidence for historical days, never today's allocation applied backward. V3 debt transitions come from DebtUpdated records; V2 debt must be read at each observation block because withdrawals can alter debt without StrategyReported. Bound every estimate by both included parent and target balances and retain cap diagnostics. Verify actual block headers: indexer day labels can differ from the true UTC block day. Reassign or patch daily observations; never relabel an after-midnight block as the prior day closing.

Daily rows represent one complete UTC day's closing holdings with observations during that day. Require every completed day of the report window, including every eligible vault. Average daily de-duplicated TVL using equal day weights. For MTD, include the current cutoff TVL with a weight equal to the elapsed fraction of the current UTC day; disclose that daily closing values approximate intraday exposure. If a period has no elapsed time, fee efficiency is unavailable. No midpoint denominator is silently substituted.

## Revenue and efficiency

Protocol revenue = fees retained by the protocol + distinct retained yLockers revenue. Gross fees = retained + distributed fees after a documented treatment of refunds. Use event-level IDs and establish recipients and retention routes; generic Transfer amounts or harvest profit are not fee revenue. Prevent double counting strategy-level fees nested inside vault fees. Distinguish treasury receipts from earned protocol revenue; sweeps and splitter payouts may lag accrual and are never additive revenue streams.

Fees per dollar = matched-period retained vault fee USD / daily average de-duplicated vault TVL USD. Units are USD/USD per period, not APY or annualized yield. Exclude yLockers from this numerator. Match denominator and fee universes exactly. Transaction IDs/log indexes and historical valuation dates back all values. If only daily quotes are available, disclose same-day closing share/asset prices; cutoff-day fees may use exact-block share conversion with the prior completed day asset quote. These are valuation conventions, not event-time spot prices.

A narrower verified-fee series may be shown as a clearly labeled lower bound when protocol-wide reported revenue is independently validated. Restrict it to share mints matched to exact harvest/report transactions and proven fee-custodian routes. Do not count custom accountant distributions funding customer yield, locked-vault rewards or user recovery as retained protocol fees. Set unavailable gross/distributed amounts to null, not an invented zero split. Unknown recipients, unpriced mints and ambiguous deposit/harvest transactions remain explicit gaps. The denominator uses the same eligible vault cohort; disclose incomplete fee-event attribution prominently. Do not label this series total fee efficiency.

## Costs and treasury

Cash spending records actual external disbursements; exclude internal transfers, swaps and receipts. Define the controlled-account scope first: funding a distributor is internal, paying an external team is cash spending. Count the same economic payment once across treasury and distributor records. Treasury receipts are shown separately from protocol revenue.

Accrual expenses allocate approved budgets by overlap of calendar days with report periods. Resolve superseded versions explicitly; reject overlapping periods for the same team/cost line. Distinct documented cost lines may coexist. Approval and cash dates are independent. New FundingDistributor approvals with unreconciled calendar-service overlap are disclosed outside the reviewed calendar-allocation chart, not forced into overlapping accrual intervals. The chart must then be labeled reviewed-scope allocation, not total accrual expense. Protocol-wide recorded costs and cash spending retain their own independent bases. Reconcile token bonuses and one-off audit/discretionary expenses as additional lines, without counting a prefund and a claim twice.

Value treasury positions at cutoff using historical prices; do not carry forward prior prices. Resolve ERC-4626 and V2 shares to base assets with block-pinned conversions. Count a share or its underlying equivalent, never both. Exclude own-token inventory and committed/earmarked assets from liquid treasury. Verify market liquidity/redemption restrictions before declaring liquidity. Unpriced positions inside the declared liquid scope block publication. A conservative liquid-stable scope may omit other inventory only with explicit position counts, reasons and an unavailable total-inventory fair value. Never treat those omitted positions as zero, describe them as worthless without evidence, or claim full treasury valuation coverage. Current token enumeration is insufficient for historical disposed holdings; reconcile token transfer history and custody completeness.

Runway = liquid treasury / latest completed quarter's average monthly gross cash spending. It assumes no revenue offset; label it as such. When protocol-wide TeamAccountant records are available, health signal = latest completed 84-day reported-period revenue / recorded costs: >=1 healthy; >=0.6 watch; <0.6 at risk. Retain those actual period boundaries; never allocate their totals into calendar months or compare them with calendar budget accrual as if equivalent. Empty current-period ledger entries mean no reports posted yet, not zero economic activity. Manual postings and bridged-receipt delays limit this accounting basis. Calendar-quarter coverage is used only when complete, independently reconstructed calendar earnings exist. Other qualitative signals remain pending unless fresh evidence supports them. These are descriptive rules, not investment guidance.

## Secondary metrics and context

Depositors require share-transfer reconstruction or complete on-chain holdings, exclusions for nested vault accounts and explicit minimum-balance threshold. Count addresses rather than people. Concentration and partner attribution must share the declared covered universe. Net deposits come from deposit/withdrawal records valued at event time and exclude internal vault migrations; TVL changes are not flows. Supply changes are issuance/redemption context; DeFi TVL changes are price sensitive stocks, not measured ecosystem flows.

Fed DFF is the average of available daily observations per month. Stablecoin supply and DeFi TVL are the latest observed stock before period end. Never substitute a current stock into every historical month. Preserve genuine missing observations as null, with coverage/source notes. Plausible monthly uniques must be queried per period rather than summed from daily uniques. GitHub repository pagination avoids Search's 1,000-result cap; private repository text stays local. Completed Linear issues are an activity measure, not a safety signal.

## Publication

Validation must establish TVL, fee retention, spending, liquid treasury and coverage with saved evidence, not self-attested totals. Reconciled inputs are the accountable research boundary: validators check consistency, units, dates and saved-evidence integrity; they cannot prove the analyst's classification is correct. Review that interpretation before publication. Preserve missing secondary values as explicit gaps. Publish aggregate HTML only, with archive retention, and retain every returned URL. The existing ChatGPT Site and October 9 report are never modified.


## Reuse and reviewed classifications

Import historical source evidence and review notes with original digests and timestamps. Refresh balances, wrappers, redeemability and price quotes at the new cutoff; scan the intervening ERC20 logs and native cash history. Imported fee-pipeline ownership fractions describe treasury economic ownership, not protocol fee retention. Calendar service periods in `budget-reviews.json` are dated reviewed requests; inspect new approvals and update those records rather than carrying them into a future term. Token symbols never prove canonical identity. APY outside −100% to +1,000% is withheld as an unverified indexed outlier; valid negative observations are preserved.
